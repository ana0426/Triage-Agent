# HackerRank Account and Login

## Login Issues

- If you cannot log in, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your password.
- Ensure you are using the correct email address associated with your account.
- If you signed up with Google or LinkedIn, use that same provider to log in — do not use email/password.
- If your account shows "Account Locked", contact HackerRank support at support@hackerrank.com with your registered email address.
- SSO (Single Sign-On) users should contact their company's IT administrator, not HackerRank support.

## Account Recovery

- Account recovery requires verification of the registered email address.
- If you no longer have access to the email, contact support with an alternative proof of identity.
- HackerRank cannot merge two separate accounts.

## Profile and Settings

- Your public HackerRank profile can be set to private in Settings > Privacy.
- Username changes are allowed once every 30 days.
- Email address changes require re-verification of the new email.

## Candidate vs Recruiter Accounts

- Candidate accounts are for job seekers completing assessments.
- Recruiter/enterprise accounts are for companies posting tests and reviewing candidates.
- Candidates cannot view other candidates' results or submissions.
- If you received an assessment link, you have a candidate view — contact the hiring company for result queries.
