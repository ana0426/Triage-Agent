# HackerRank Scoring and Results

## How Scores Are Calculated

- Scores are based on the number of test cases passed, code correctness, and sometimes time/space complexity.
- Each problem has its own point value set by the recruiter.
- Partial credit may be given for partially passing solutions depending on the test configuration.
- Submission time and number of attempts may be factored in for tie-breaking, depending on recruiter settings.

## Score Disputes

- Scores are considered final unless a technical issue is reported within 24 hours of test completion.
- To dispute a score, you must provide evidence of a technical failure (screenshot, network error, etc.).
- HackerRank support cannot modify or override scores — only the recruiter/hiring company can reset a test.
- If you believe a test case is incorrect, escalate to the hiring company, not HackerRank support.

## Recruiter Access to Results

- Recruiters control visibility of your results. HackerRank cannot share your score directly if the recruiter has restricted access.
- To get your score, contact the company that sent you the assessment.

## Plagiarism and Code Similarity

- HackerRank uses automated plagiarism detection. Flags are based on code similarity with other submissions.
- If you believe a plagiarism flag is incorrect, the recruiter or hiring company can request a manual review.
- HackerRank support does not override recruiter plagiarism decisions.
- Contact the hiring company directly regarding plagiarism flags.

## Certification Tests

- HackerRank certifications are available for Python, Java, SQL, JavaScript, and other skills.
- You may retake certification tests after a mandatory waiting period (varies by certification).
- Certificates are issued automatically when you pass and can be shared via your HackerRank profile URL.
- For employer verification of a certificate, share your public profile link.
