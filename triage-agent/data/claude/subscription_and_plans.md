# Claude Subscriptions and Plans

## Claude Free

- Claude Free is available at claude.ai at no cost.
- Free users have a daily message limit that resets every 24 hours.
- Free users may be limited to Claude 3 Haiku during high-demand periods.
- Free accounts do not include priority access or advanced features.

## Claude Pro

- Claude Pro is a monthly subscription at $20/month (or equivalent in local currency).
- Subscribe at claude.ai/upgrade.
- Pro benefits: higher message limits, priority access during peak times, access to Claude 3.5 Sonnet and Opus, longer context windows.
- Pro billing is monthly. Cancel anytime at claude.ai/settings/billing.
- Cancelling stops renewal but does not refund partial months. Contact support@anthropic.com for refund requests — refunds are not guaranteed.

## Claude for Teams

- Team plans offer shared usage, admin controls, and centralized billing.
- Team pricing starts at $25/user/month (minimum 5 seats).
- Contact sales@anthropic.com for team plan setup.

## Subscription Issues

- If you were charged but cannot access Pro features, log out and log back in to refresh your subscription status.
- If you were billed twice in one month, contact support@anthropic.com immediately with your payment details.
- Subscription cancellation confirmation is sent via email. If you did not receive it, check your spam folder or contact support.
