# Claude Account Access and Privacy

## Logging In

- Visit claude.ai and sign in with your email, or via Google or Apple.
- If you signed up with Google/Apple, you must use the same provider — email/password login will not work.
- Use "Forgot Password" to reset your password if you signed up with email.
- Account lockouts (after multiple failed login attempts) are temporary. Wait 15 minutes before retrying.
- If you cannot log in after a password reset, contact support@anthropic.com with your account email.

## Account Recovery

- Account recovery requires access to the registered email address.
- If you no longer control the registered email, Anthropic may require additional identity verification.
- Account deletion requests are handled at support@anthropic.com. Deletion is permanent and irreversible.

## Data Privacy

- By default, conversations with Claude may be reviewed by Anthropic to improve safety and performance.
- You can opt out of data training in Settings > Privacy on claude.ai.
- Anthropic does not sell personal data to third parties.
- For GDPR or CCPA data deletion requests, email privacy@anthropic.com. Include your account email and specify the request type.
- Data retention policy: conversations are stored for up to 30 days by default (configurable for enterprise).

## Safety and Content Policy

- Claude is designed with safety guidelines. It will refuse harmful, illegal, or unethical requests.
- If Claude refuses a legitimate request, try rephrasing or providing more context.
- To report a safety concern or policy violation, email safety@anthropic.com.
