# Visa Fraud, Disputes, and Chargebacks

## Unauthorized Transactions

- If you see a transaction you do not recognize, first check whether a family member may have made the purchase.
- Report unauthorized transactions to your issuing bank immediately — do not wait.
- Most banks have a 24/7 fraud hotline (number on the back of your card).
- Visa's Zero Liability Policy: you are not responsible for unauthorized Visa card transactions, subject to timely reporting and your bank's terms.
- Do not share your card number, CVV, PIN, or OTP with anyone — including people claiming to be Visa or bank staff.

## Chargeback Process

- A chargeback is a reversal of a transaction, initiated through your bank.
- Valid chargeback reasons include: item not received, item significantly not as described, unauthorized transaction, merchant failed to process a refund, duplicate charge.
- Contact your issuing bank to initiate a chargeback. Visa's Dispute Resolution (Visa Resolve Online) is managed by banks, not directly by customers.
- Gather supporting evidence: receipts, email correspondence, photos, screenshots.
- File a dispute within 120 days of the transaction date.
- Chargeback resolution can take 30 to 90 days. Your bank may issue provisional credit during investigation.

## Fraud Reporting

- For active fraud (someone is using your card right now), call your bank's fraud line immediately.
- To report a phishing scam or fraudulent website using the Visa brand, email phishing@visa.com.
- Never provide card details to someone who called you unsolicited, even if they claim to be your bank or Visa.

## Transaction Disputes with Merchants

- For a billing error or merchant dispute (wrong amount, double charge, subscription not cancelled), contact the merchant first.
- If the merchant does not resolve it, contact your bank to initiate a dispute.
- Keep records of all communication with the merchant as evidence.
