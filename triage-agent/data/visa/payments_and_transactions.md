# Visa Payments and Transactions

## Contactless Payments

- Visa contactless payments use NFC (Near Field Communication) technology.
- Tap your Visa card or device on the contactless-enabled terminal (look for the wave symbol).
- Transaction limits for contactless payments vary by country and bank (typically $25–$100 without PIN).
- Higher amounts may require chip insertion and PIN.
- If contactless is not working, insert the chip or swipe the magnetic stripe.
- Contact your bank to enable or disable contactless on your card.

## Online Payments

- Visa Secure (formerly Verified by Visa / 3D Secure) adds an extra authentication step for online transactions.
- During Visa Secure, you may receive an OTP (One-Time Password) to your registered mobile number.
- If you did not receive the OTP, contact your bank to update your registered mobile number.
- Never enter your OTP on a site that wasn't initiated by you.

## International Transactions

- International purchases are converted using Visa's exchange rate, which is competitive and publicly available at Visa.com.
- Your bank may charge a foreign transaction fee (typically 1–3%) on top of the exchange rate.
- Some banks offer zero-foreign-transaction-fee cards — check with your bank.
- Notify your bank before traveling to avoid blocks on international usage.

## Recurring Payments and Subscriptions

- To cancel a recurring charge (subscription), contact the merchant first.
- If the merchant does not cancel the subscription, contact your bank to block future charges.
- Visa cannot cancel subscriptions on your behalf — this is handled by the merchant or your bank.

## Payment Not Processed

- If a payment was not processed but the amount was reserved (pending), this may be a pre-authorization hold. It typically releases within 3–7 business days.
- If a payment failed but was debited, contact your bank immediately with the transaction details.
