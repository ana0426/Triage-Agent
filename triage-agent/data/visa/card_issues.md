# Visa Card Issues

## Card Declined

- Visa does not directly manage individual card accounts — your issuing bank does.
- If your Visa card is declined, contact your bank using the number on the back of your card.
- Common reasons for decline: insufficient funds, incorrect PIN, expired card, international use not enabled, merchant restrictions, or suspected fraud.
- Visa cannot override a decline decision — this must be handled by your bank.

## Card Blocked While Traveling

- Banks may block international transactions as a fraud prevention measure.
- Always notify your issuing bank before traveling internationally.
- Visa Global Customer Assistance: 1-800-847-2911 (available 24/7 worldwide, toll-free in most countries).
- Your bank can unblock international usage remotely via phone or their app.
- Keep your bank's emergency contact number saved separately from your wallet.

## Lost or Stolen Card

- Report a lost or stolen card to your issuing bank immediately.
- Your bank will cancel the old card and issue a replacement.
- Visa's Zero Liability Policy protects you from unauthorized charges on lost/stolen Visa cards (subject to your bank's specific terms and timely reporting).
- For emergency cash abroad, contact your bank or use Visa's Emergency Cash Disbursement service via Visa Global Customer Assistance.

## Card Not Arriving

- New and replacement card delivery times depend on your bank, not Visa.
- Contact your bank if your card has not arrived within the expected timeframe.
- Expedited delivery may be available for an additional fee from your bank.

## ATM Issues

- Visa cards work at ATMs globally through the Visa/Plus network. Look for the Visa or Plus logo.
- If an ATM retains (swallows) your card, contact your bank immediately.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limits are set by your bank, not Visa.
- ATM fees are set by the ATM operator and your bank — Visa does not charge ATM fees.
- If a foreign ATM offers currency conversion ("Do you want to be charged in your home currency?"), declining usually results in better exchange rates.
